Trees: council street-tree inventories that publish species, from City of Sydney and Randwick City Council (pulled 4 Nov 2026), plus jacarandas tagged in OpenStreetMap and sightings logged on iNaturalist across the rest of Sydney. Those two aren't full inventories, so suburbs outside the two councils are under-counted, and a few well-known spots are added by hand. Basemap: roads, parks and rail © OpenStreetMap contributors (Oct 2026 extract), shoreline from NSW suburb boundaries. Weather and clouds: Open-Meteo forecast on a grid about 11 km apart. The clouds on the map follow the forecast cover for the hour you pick; their shapes are drawn, not satellite images. Sun positions are calculated in your browser. If the forecast can't load, set the sky by hand from the BOM forecast.
